# Phoenix icons and README covers (2026-11-04)

The owner is renaming the project to TaroBird and replaced the fox with an
owner-selected chubby phoenix (light-purple lower tail flame). This change
covers images only:

- App icons on every platform (macOS ICNS, Windows ICO/PNGs, iOS AppIcon and
  launch mark, Android legacy/round/adaptive launchers, favicon and touch
  icon) and the in-App scene mark, all regenerated by
  `scripts/generate-app-icons.mjs` from `assets/brand/launcher-art.png` and
  `assets/brand/phoenix-art.png`. The launcher background is now `#e2dad3`,
  the phoenix art's own warm gray.
- README covers (`assets/tarobot-cover*.png`, `scripts/render-readme-art.mjs
  --covers-only`) and the social banner (`assets/github-banner.svg/png`) show
  the phoenix and the **TaroBird** wordmark.

Unchanged and still to do separately: the product name in App text, package
names and identifiers (still TaroBot); the Android monochrome/notification
glyph and CLI-style scenes (`fox-monochrome.svg`, a fox silhouette); the
macOS status item (`tray-macos.svg`, a cat-head mask); the Bot avatar library,
including `pastel-fox` as the first Bot's default avatar.

Verification: `python3 scripts/verify-release-icons.py --source` passes
(84 generated files, Android eyes inside the safe circle, iOS launch parity).
The brand contact sheet from `scripts/render-brand-preview.mjs` is a preview
of generated resources, not evidence of an installed App or a physical device.
No Host/CLI behaviour, schema or settings change.
